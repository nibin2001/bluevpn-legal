Delete your data

You can ask us to delete all the data Blue VPN Pro holds about you at any time. It's free and takes a few minutes.

How to request deletion

  1. Open your Google Play purchase receipt email, or Google Play → Payments & subscriptions → Budget & history, and find your order number. It starts with GPA.
  2. Email bluevpnpro.teamsupport@gmail.com with the subject “Delete my data” and include that order number.
  3. We confirm by email and delete your data within 30 days. No other identification is needed.

Deleting your data does not cancel your subscription. To stop future charges, cancel in Google Play → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions → Blue VPN Pro.

To remove the data stored on your phone, uninstall Blue VPN Pro or clear its storage in Android Settings → Apps → Blue VPN Pro → Storage.

What is kept

  • Your deletion request email, kept for up to 12 months as a record that we completed it, then deleted.
  • Payment and order records held by Google Play. Google processes all payments and keeps its own records under the Google Privacy Policy. We cannot delete those.

We never record your browsing activity, DNS queries or connection history, so there is none to delete.
