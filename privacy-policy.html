Privacy Policy

Blue VPN Pro ("we", "us", or "our"), engineered and operated by the Blue VPN Security & Engineering Team, is dedicated to safeguarding user privacy, network security, and digital freedom. This Privacy Policy outlines our transparent data handling and no-logs practices across the Blue VPN Pro Android application and our global encrypted server infrastructure.

Core Zero-Logs Principle: We do not log, track, monitor, or store your internet activity, browsing history, destination URLs, source IP addresses, connection timestamps, or DNS queries. What you do online stays entirely private.

1. Strict Zero-Logs Architecture

Our server daemons execute strictly in volatile physical RAM buffers with persistent disk logging completely disabled:

  • NO Browsing History: We never record, inspect, or intercept the websites you access or data packets transmitted.
  • NO Originating IP Logging: We do not store your real-world IP address before, during, or after VPN sessions.
  • NO Timestamps: We do not record connection timestamps, session durations, or session start/stop events.
  • NO DNS Logging: All DNS lookups are routed through private local encrypted resolvers (1.1.1.1 / 1.0.0.1) and immediately discarded.
  • NO Bandwidth Profiling: We do not build user behavioral profiles or throttle connections based on traffic type.

2. Use of Android VpnService API

Blue VPN Pro utilizes Android's native VpnService API solely to establish a secure, encrypted WireGuard® tunnel to our global servers (India, Singapore, Germany, and worldwide nodes).

  • Encrypts outbound and inbound network packets using ChaCha20-Poly1305 authenticated encryption with Curve25519 key exchange.
  • Enforces Fail-Safe Kill Switch protection to block unencrypted leakages when network handoffs happen.
  • Prevents DNS leaks by forcing private DNS routing inside the tunnel.
  • The VpnService is never used to inject ads, capture unencrypted payloads, or monitor your web traffic.

3. Information We Process

A. Subscriptions & Billing Data

All subscriptions, monthly renewals, and 7-day free trials are processed directly and securely by Google Play Billing. We do not receive, process, or store credit card numbers, payment card details, or banking credentials. A cryptographically hashed purchase token is stored locally on your device via Android KeyStore (AES-256 GCM) solely to confirm active subscription entitlement.

B. Installed Apps (Split Tunneling)

When utilizing the Split Tunneling feature, the app queries the local list of installed applications on your device. This list is processed entirely on-device and is never transmitted or uploaded to external servers.

C. Anonymous Crash Diagnostics

Non-identifiable aggregated crash telemetry provided automatically by Android OS (such as OS version and stack traces) may be used to fix application bugs. No network traffic or personal identifiers are contained in these reports.

4. Multi-Device Protection & Fair Use

  • Unlimited Devices: Subscribers can protect multiple personal devices under their active subscription without artificial device capping.
  • Acceptable Use: The service is provided strictly for lawful personal security and privacy. Port scanning, botnets, DDoS attacks, or distribution of illicit materials is strictly prohibited.

5. Third-Party Services

  • Google Play Billing Library: Exclusively for verifying subscriptions and restoring purchases. For Google's data handling, see Google Privacy Policy.

6. Security & Infrastructure Hardening

  • Hardware Encryption: Local preferences and session states are secured using hardware-backed Android Keystore (AES-256 GCM).
  • Server Perimeters: Nodes operate on hardened Linux kernels with disabled root access, strict UFW firewalls, and Fail2Ban intrusion detection.

7. GDPR & CCPA User Rights

Under GDPR, CCPA, and global privacy standards, you have the right to data erasure and privacy verification. Because Blue VPN Pro adheres to a strict zero-logs policy, we do not possess any personal browsing records to disclose, export, or monetize. You can erase local cached application state at any time by clearing application data or uninstalling the app.
