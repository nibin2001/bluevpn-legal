Privacy Policy

Effective November 8, 2026

Blue VPN Pro (“we”, “us”) provides a WireGuard®-based VPN app for Android. This policy explains what the app and our servers process, why, and for how long. We built the service to need as little information as possible.

We never log

  • Websites you visit or apps you use
  • The content of your traffic
  • Your DNS queries
  • Connection history or bandwidth use

We do process

  • A random account ID (no name or email)
  • Your Google Play subscription status
  • Device model and last-active time
  • Temporary session data while connected

1. No-logs commitment

When your traffic passes through a Blue VPN Pro server, we do not record:

  • the websites, services or apps you use, or the content of your traffic;
  • the destination IP addresses you connect to, or your DNS queries;
  • your originating IP address together with your VPN activity;
  • a history of your connections, session times or the amount of data you transfer.

Our account server runs with request logging turned off, and the HTTPS proxy in front of it has access logging disabled. While you are connected, the VPN server necessarily holds your current IP address in memory so that it can send traffic back to you. It is discarded when your session ends and is never written to a log.

To run a paid service, we keep a small amount of account and device information. None of it is linked to what you do online.

If you contact us or use the in-app feedback form, we receive what you choose to send: your email address, the topic and your message. The app contains no analytics or advertising SDKs. If you have chosen to share diagnostics with Google, Google Play may give us anonymous crash reports (device model, Android version and technical error details). These never include network or browsing information.

3. Data that stays on your device

  • Your WireGuard private key is generated on your device and never leaves it. Only the matching public key is sent to our servers.
  • Settings (protocol, kill switch, DNS, auto-connect and split-tunnelling choices) are stored locally and encrypted with the Android Keystore.
  • Installed apps: the split-tunnelling screen reads the list of apps on your device so you can choose which ones use the VPN. This list is never sent anywhere.
  • Wi-Fi state: if you turn on auto-connect for unsecured Wi-Fi, the app checks the security of the network you join. This check happens only on your device.

4. How we use Android’s VpnService

Blue VPN Pro uses Android’s VpnService API for one purpose: to create an encrypted WireGuard tunnel (ChaCha20-Poly1305) between your device and the Blue VPN Pro server you choose. Your DNS queries travel inside that tunnel and are resolved by Cloudflare’s 1.1.1.1 resolver, or by DNS servers you set yourself, which prevents DNS leaks.

We never use VpnService to collect personal or sensitive data, inject advertisements, redirect your traffic, build browsing profiles, or sell data.

6. Legal bases for processing

Where laws such as the EU or UK GDPR apply, we rely on:

  • Performance of a contract: account, subscription, device and session data needed to provide the VPN you signed up for;
  • Legitimate interests: keeping the service secure and preventing abuse;
  • Your request or consent: replying to support messages you send us.

7. International transfers

Our servers are in India, Singapore and Germany, and our providers may process data in other countries, including the United States. Where required, we rely on our providers’ standard contractual clauses or similar safeguards.

8. Security

  • All VPN traffic is encrypted end to end between your device and our server using WireGuard.
  • The app talks to our account servers only over HTTPS, and checks each server’s certificate against a key built into the app.
  • App data on your device is encrypted with the Android Keystore.
  • Our servers accept administrator logins by SSH key only, are protected by firewalls, and run the account service with minimal privileges.

No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect the little data we hold.

10. Your rights and data deletion

  • Delete your data: email bluevpnpro.teamsupport@gmail.com with the subject “Delete my data” and your Google Play order number (it starts with “GPA.” and is in your Google Play purchase receipt). We delete your account, devices and subscription records within 30 days. No other identification is needed.
  • Remove local data: clear the app’s storage or uninstall the app.
  • Cancel your subscription: at any time in Google Play → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions. Deleting your data does not cancel a subscription by itself.

Depending on where you live, for example under the GDPR or California law, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or receive a copy of your data, to object to or restrict processing, and to complain to your local data protection authority. Email us to use any of these rights; we reply within 30 days.

11. Children

Blue VPN Pro is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them. If you believe a child has used the service, contact us and we will delete the related data.
